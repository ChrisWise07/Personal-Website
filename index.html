In Progress
Quantum Computing Transformers Noise Modelling

Transformer-based Qubit Simulator

An encoder-only transformer integrated into an open-quantum system qubit simulator. The model learns to map Gaussian white noise to realistic correlated quantum computer noise. Achieves 99.97% accuracy on ground truth expectations, with backpropagated control pulses attaining 99.89% process fidelity in noisy environments.

Publication Forthcoming
Quantum Computing Regression ML arXiv:2505.03397

Quantum Feature Space of a Qubit Coupled to an Arbitrary Bath

Demonstrates that quantum noise operators can be directly, accurately, and efficiently inferred using regression-based approaches compared to deep learning methods. Formulates an efficient parameterisation of the noise operator description, effective at characterising complex qubit-bath interactions.

Honours Thesis Deep Learning Optimal Control

Machine Learning for High Fidelity Qubit Control

First-Class Honours thesis extending physical mathematical models of qubit-environment interactions to infer system noise from real experimental data. Developed deep learning models mapping control pulses to interactions, utilising gradient-free optimisers to synthesise pulses achieving over 99% process fidelity.

Quantum Circuits PSO Algorithm Error Mitigation

PSO to Infer Quantum Computer Circuit Errors

Quantum computing circuit error parameters were deduced using Particle Swarm Optimisation (PSO) to refine a circuit error simulation model. Reduced mean squared error between model simulations and hardware data by a magnitude of 10x, enabling control calibrations that suppressed measured errors by 62%.

Developing Decentralised Resilience to Malicious Influence

Investigated collective perception and opinion fusion in multi-agent swarms under Byzantine and adversarial attacks. Developed reinforcement learning agents that dynamically weight received peer opinions, achieving robust decentralised consensus in Python simulation environments.
